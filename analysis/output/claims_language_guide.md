# Claims Language Guide

## Use (Recommended)
- "associated with"
- "tracks higher fragility risk"
- "directionally robust across specifications"
- "marker of instability risk"
- "attenuated under conservative-null checks"

## Avoid
- "causes fragility"
- "proves mechanism"
- "establishes causal effect"
- "definitive driver"

## Abstract Template Sentence
In Cochrane pairwise meta-analyses, higher assumption debt was associated with higher fragility risk across multiple dependence- and threshold-sensitive specifications, with conservative-null analyses indicating residual attribution uncertainty.

## Discussion Guardrail Sentence
These findings support interpreting assumption debt as a practical risk-stratification marker for fragility, not as a standalone causal mechanism.
