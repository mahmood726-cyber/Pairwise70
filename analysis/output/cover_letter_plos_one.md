# Cover Letter (Target: PLOS ONE)

Dear Editors,

We submit the manuscript "Assumption debt and fragility of conclusions in Cochrane pairwise meta-analyses: A retrospective methodological study" for consideration at PLOS ONE.

This study evaluates whether a composite assumption-burden measure is associated with fragility of conclusions in 4,424 Cochrane pairwise meta-analyses. We use dependence-aware inference, extensive robustness analyses, and transparent conservative-null checks. The key result is a consistent positive association between assumption debt and fragility risk across model families, while explicitly bounding interpretation where conservative checks attenuate inference.

We believe the manuscript fits PLOS ONE’s scope by providing a reproducible, methodologically rigorous analysis with broad relevance to evidence synthesis practice.

The submission is original, not under consideration elsewhere, and approved by all authors.
