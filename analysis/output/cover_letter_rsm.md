# Cover Letter (Target: Research Synthesis Methods)

Dear Editors,

We submit our manuscript, "Assumption Debt and Fragility in Cochrane Pairwise Meta-Analyses," for consideration at *Research Synthesis Methods*.

This paper addresses a methodological gap central to evidence synthesis practice: assumption burden is rarely quantified as an explicit inferential dimension. Using 4,424 Cochrane pairwise meta-analyses (Pairwise70), we operationalize assumption debt and evaluate its association with fragility of conclusions under extensive dependence-aware and specification-aware sensitivity analyses.

Our main contribution is methodological rather than purely applied. We provide:
- a reproducible framework for quantifying assumption burden,
- a multiverse robustness synthesis (including specification curve and directional sign testing), and
- calibrated interpretation rules that distinguish robust directional evidence from mechanistic over-claiming.

Key findings include a positive adjusted association between assumption debt and fragility (cluster-robust OR 1.15, 95% CI 1.02 to 1.30), persistence across assessability-restricted analyses (k >= 10 subset cluster-robust OR 1.31, 95% CI 1.07 to 1.61), and strong cross-specification directional concordance (51/52 checks positive; one-sided sign-test p = 1.18e-14). Conservative permutation sensitivity (empirical p = 0.08) is reported transparently and used to bound interpretation.

The manuscript aligns with *RSM* priorities by emphasizing methodological transparency, dependence handling, robustness synthesis, and guidance for responsible inference language in applied meta-analysis.

This work is original, not under consideration elsewhere, and all authors approve submission.
